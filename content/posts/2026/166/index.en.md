Diablo 2 in real life

Sometimes, to keep my mental health in order and to keep my brain from leaking out, I play games. Not often, but it happens. At the moment I'm playing Hearthstone, and not long ago I got my hands on Diablo 2 Remastered with updated graphics. My nostalgic taste buds are in ecstasy.

So, here's the thing. In Act 1 the locations are swampy plains and the like. In Act 2 the action moves to Lut Gholein. Given that I moved from St. Petersburg to Izmir, my change of locations gives me a persistent association with the Diablo acts and some unhealthy giggling.

And I'm really curious what's in store for real life in Act 3?
