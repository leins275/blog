An automatic word processor

Artificial intelligence is a bad name for the technology. It confuses people and invites references to the film "Terminator". It splits people into two types. Some go into hard denial, others into euphoria at finally getting at least some intelligence of their own.

Both of these approaches seem ineffective to me, and it all comes from the name.

But if it had been given some dull name, say, "automatic word processor". And that's it, the problem is solved! Nobody would run around implementing AI in their businesses, and everyone would use it casually, like just another program from an office suite.

But then, of course, there would be no marketing...
