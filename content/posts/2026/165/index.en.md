The Eternal Shopping List

Many people find it useful to "do a walk-through" in a grocery store. That way, you can look at all the available products and remember what you need to buy. This works because going through all the options is usually easier in this case than preparing the needed list in advance.

However, lately I have been ordering groceries online much more often. Wandering around a store isn't possible, and digging through an app isn't as convenient.

So for myself I came up with a simulation: an eternal shopping list. It includes not only the products I need to buy today, but everything I buy at least occasionally. It works wonderfully. It's enough to go through the list from top to bottom and pick what's needed for today. It takes less energy to cut out the unnecessary than to recall what's needed.
