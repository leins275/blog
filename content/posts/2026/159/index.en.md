Readonly and Writeonly tools

Different tools are good for different tasks. The idea seems obvious, doesn't it?

I propose the following classification of tools: tools where it's convenient to write, and tools where it's convenient to read. The funny thing is that these are almost always different things.

Take, for example, you as a manager who wants to assign a task. To do it, you can open Telegram and type three words into a chat with the developers. For you, this is the best scenario: it requires the least effort.

If you're a developer to whom this wonderful person from the paragraph above assigns a task, you don't want to communicate with them that way. You already have 50+ chats, and in each one someone clever wants something, while in between, the aesthetes discuss a 1986 vintage Sauvignon Blanc. If you're a developer, you want to receive not a message in a chat, but a ticket in Jira, containing only the context you need, nothing extra, and everything properly laid out.

In my view, one good application of AI is ensuring integration between tools for writing and tools for reading. Everyone uses the tool that's convenient for their task, and everyone is happy.
