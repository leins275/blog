Claude Code, TV shows and Turkish

I decided to try yet another approach to learning Turkish. I can already say "hi-bye-thanks" for sure, but I want to tackle trickier questions.

Here's how I entertain myself on vacation. My wife and I downloaded a TV show in Turkish with English subtitles. We watch it with the original audio and read the English subtitles.

For the first pass, Claude wrote a Python script to generate native Turkish subtitles via Whisper. On my MacBook Air with 16 GB and an M4 chip, processing 10 episodes with the local whisper-large model takes about 70 minutes. Quite acceptable. Now we watch with two subtitle tracks - IINA player on Mac can do that, I recommend it. (This is purely for my own fun; my wife has already reached B1 at minimum on her own through YouTube and Duolingo, what a scary person.)

Then we make a second pass. We ask Claude to analyze the subs, use a script to count bigrams, trigrams and 4-grams, sort them by frequency, and build Anki decks by week using best practices of the lexical approach to language learning.

Now I have 12 sets of wonderful flashcards, each set covering 1 week. An example card is in the screenshot. It's funny that now even a slacker and C student like me, knowing just 2.5 clever words and having Claude Code, can do such crazy stuff... Pure bliss.
