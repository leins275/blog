The Basics of Good Rest

This New Year I was on vacation for 2 weeks. I ended up resting remarkably well. It would be great to repeat that... But how? Can you rest well on purpose? I don't like it when things happen by chance. I like being able to get what I need deliberately and at the right moment. And for that, it's important to understand the mechanics of good rest.

I really liked this video - https://youtu.be/gKXJQLg1-cg?si=LOprHArVKU5u3A8t. Fatigue can be divided into physical, emotional, and mental. I was mainly interested in mental fatigue. As advice, the video suggests various activities: taking a walk, washing dishes, knitting, and others. This raises the question: is switching activities enough for good mental rest? The answer is no. Another necessary condition for quality rest is mindfulness, that is, a state of full presence in the moment.

Neuroscience identifies two main neural networks in our brain. The TPN (Task Positive Network) activates when we are actively solving some problems. The DMN (Default Mode Network), on the other hand, works when we are at mental rest. As comrade Lenin said, rest is a change of activity. For the brain to rest, you need to switch between the DMN and the TPN. And to do that, you need not just to change activities, but to do it mindfully. If during a walk you keep actively thinking about solving work problems, or if during lunch you watch videos, a full switch doesn't happen. That's why I'm now actively trying to build up my mindfulness skill.
