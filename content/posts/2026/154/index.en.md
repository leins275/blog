My stages of using AI

1. Some shady crap. I won't bother with it, I'll wait until it "airs out."

2. Chatbot. Oh, neat. It answers questions! Sometimes it even guesses right, how lovely.

3. GitHub Copilot. When someone edits files for you, that's convenient! Claude Sonnet seems to be the most normal of them...

4. Claude Code. There's not much difference, but it's the lawgiver of mods, so I'll stick around here. Supposedly you need to write all kinds of skills, but how do you manage them afterward... I'll just prompt.

5. Claude Code marketplace. Wait, you could do that?! Pirated plugins from other marketplaces, my first plugin. Why make a report in one evening when you can spend three days generating it.
I love the smell of automation napalm in the mornings.

6. Orchestration. Took a couple of Anthropic courses. Got the idea of multi-agent setups. It's all just like with people. No autonomy and no democracy. Only absolute monarchy with a single coordinator tsar.

7. SDD - accumulating a critical mass of information. Karpathy's LLM wiki, a logbook, specifications, spec-kit. Shiny trinkets, but how do you actually use them?

8. First approaches to SDD - 120 tickets from an analyst turned into real features in production in 2 weeks.

9. Ahh, I figured it out! My first SDD plugin. A skill orchestrator for the SDD workflow, skills and plugin dependencies. Descriptions of various subagents. SDLC TDD dark factory in full bloom. The full harness. Versioning and delivery through the marketplace for the entire company.

-> This is where I'm hanging out...
