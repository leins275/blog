Bookmarks in the browser

Google Chrome has gotten really aggressive, so I had to dig out my old trusty Firefox. While I was tinkering with it, I found this neat little tool https://floccus.org/ for syncing bookmarks.

Yes, you can import bookmarks directly... but if you're as weird as I am and want to store your bookmarks in Git, then I recommend it. You get both syncing and versioning.
