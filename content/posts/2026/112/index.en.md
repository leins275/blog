Open Source VibeCode

Here I want to share my vibecoded project that I use almost every day.

First, a transcriber. Google Meet doesn't want to transcribe in Russian, and Yandex Telemost at best manages to record a call. So now I have my own fully local transcriber, which transcribes, splits the speakers, generates summaries, compresses videos, and also helps me sort meetings into projects. Right now I have 91 recordings and 11 projects, and after I added video compression, the whole thing weighs 28 gigabytes.

Second, I vibecoded literally today from a simple spec a tool with a telling name, eye-saver. It's something like a Pomodoro timer, but its main purpose is to give your eyes a break, the 20-20-20 rule and all that. There are plenty of programs like this, but now I have a custom and pretty one)

Both tools can be downloaded straight from GitHub and tested. There are Windows builds, which is mainly what I use myself, and the transcriber is also available for Mac, but the Mac version is poorly tested.

What are you vibecoding? Share in the comments!
