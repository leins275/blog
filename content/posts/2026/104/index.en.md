An Unusual Benefit of AI Agents

Right now AI agents are the new black. A very useful and groundbreaking technology, yet there is far more hype around it than real benefit. Although the benefit is enormous. While everyone is trying to figure out whose job the soulless hunk of iron will take tomorrow, I look at agents from a different angle.

It seems to me that studying the principles of working with agents can help us better understand ourselves. After all, in a sense, we are AI agents too...

When you try to explain the principles of effective mental work to people, you run into a lot of misunderstanding, resistance and skepticism. Like, I already know it all myself, I handle multitasking just fine, this is my creative mess, and other nonsense.

And now - how wonderful. The whole world is buzzing and talking about limiting the context of agents, about the effectiveness of multi-agent systems built on the division of responsibility and focus. About how an agent without sufficient context won't get anything done. About the importance of precise and concise instructions and examples.

I think that when we as humanity realize the basic principles of working effectively with agents, we will also come to understand ourselves better. The analogy is perfectly straightforward. And maybe we will stop trying to solve all problems at once and act without sufficient context.
