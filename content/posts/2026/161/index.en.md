What do you think about AI-generated music?  

Here's how I listen to music. I go through my wave on Yandex Music, like the tracks that sound decent. Then, from the ones I've liked, I periodically make playlists, and those already contain more "selected" tracks.  

Recently I came across a couple of tracks that I singled out for myself as very good, standing out in my wave, let's say. Since I know that nothing gets done these days without neural networks, I did some research on these two songs. The result is this: the author writes the lyrics, and then neural networks generate both the music and the vocals. And such songs easily win my attention in my wave.  

In 30–40 years, those who sing themselves and play musical instruments will be "niche."
