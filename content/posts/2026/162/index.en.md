Do it averagely

What is the value of cleaning? Is it to make things clean? Of course not. The point of cleaning is to keep things from being dirty.

In the case of cleaning, the Pareto principle reveals itself in full. If I can get 80% of the result for 20% of the effort, that is more than acceptable. I need to forbid myself from doing better. After all, the more effort I spend today, the harder it is for me to start next time. I immediately picture the scale of the job in my head, and my strength melts away. Regularity beats quality, and regularity needs an easy start. Efficiency provides that.

And this works not only with cleaning. Studying, keeping good physical shape, cooking food. There is a huge layer of tasks where the main value is preserving the drive for the next repetition.

The point is not even in the specific activities, but in your attitude toward them. If you love cooking and it is your hobby, you can and should bring every dish to perfection. If the task is a regular delivery to yourself of a Harvard plate three times a day, it is better to buy groceries and prepare ingredients in advance. In short, cut corners.
