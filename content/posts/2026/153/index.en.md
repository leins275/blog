The product approach of a healthy person

Usually, when a product gains a certain number of users, it starts getting stuffed with new features. Often these features are controversial, cause negativity among users, and get in the way of the tool doing its main job.

I believe that strong teams can not only add features, but also remove them! The ability to realize that not every idea is good, and the willingness to throw out a finished feature from beta if its value is unclear, is honorable and respected.
