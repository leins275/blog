At first I used the Forest app. But to use it, you absolutely need a phone, and you have to constantly switch the tracking mode... All in all, not very convenient. So I wondered about it and eventually came to Toggl Track. I tracked what I was doing throughout the day the whole of September, and the experience has been extremely positive. And if we take as a kind of norm 4 hours of focused time per day, which you can hear about in many places, then an average of 6 hours a day, even allowing for errors, looks quite solid.

Pros of Toggl over Forest? It works in the browser, and it's very convenient to start a timer for any activity. I divided all the activities I'm interested in tracking into 5 projects, with subcategories in the form of tags. You can track how much time gets wasted on work calls, how much time is spent on a particular project. I like the reports tab; you can feel that someone thought about UX.

In general, logging is a fairly fascinating process. It lets you retrospectively assess the real state of affairs. The main thing is not to show it to your employer, or he might fall into a stupor from the fact that in reality it's not 40 hours a week spent on continuous firefighting)
