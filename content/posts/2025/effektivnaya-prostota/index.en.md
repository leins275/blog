In 1928, Atatürk changed the Turkish writing system from Arabic script to Latin script. This had many goals, but the main one was simplifying the writing system in order to make learning far more efficient on a mass scale.

It seems to me that this is an excellent example of how simplification lets us move forward.

Wise simplification does not simplify thinking itself. On the contrary, it allows us to shift focus from mechanical and secondary details to what is essential.
