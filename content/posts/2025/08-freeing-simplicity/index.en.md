In 1928, Atatürk changed the Turkish writing system from Arabic script to Latin. This had many goals, but the main one was simplification in order to massively increase efficiency.

It seems to me that this is an excellent example of how simplification allows us to move forward.

A (well-executed) simplification does not reduce the overall complexity of thinking. On the contrary, it allows us to shift focus from mechanical and secondary details to what is essential.
