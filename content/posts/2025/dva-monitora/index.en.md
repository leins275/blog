As soon as I arrived in Turkey, I started setting up my workspace. I've always taken this issue seriously. But since I didn't know how long I would be staying here, I decided to buy a simple full HD monitor. The main criterion was the size of the stand, so it wouldn't take up much space on the desk.

But recently I decided it was time to experience the full experience and bought a bigger monitor as a replacement. I went from 24 inches to 27, and from full HD to 4K. And since in Turkey you can only sell something normally for free, I decided not to sell the old monitor, but to buy an additional wall mount for two monitors and try using both at once.

What can I say - how convenient it is! The main feature of using several monitors is using one of them as the main one and the second as an auxiliary. But for me, of course, the key feature is the ability to work in parallel with VDI in an enterprise environment and on a local computer. Switching on one monitor is nowhere near as convenient, and this can't be solved even with virtual desktops, since the hotkeys for switching won't work.

All in all, I recommend it to everyone
