I increasingly notice that I use only the Downloads folder. Let's say, in 90% of cases. If grouping files by creation date is enabled, such an organization turns out to be quite sufficient.

Well, in general, I've reflected on the fact that grouping things by date is very convenient. For example, now in all my notes I reserve the first-level heading for the date. For compactness, I record the date in the format 251018 (25 year, 10 month, 18 day). This makes it easy to develop the thought further in the future, preserving the history in a convenient-to-view form.

Well, and in all my notebooks, of course, I also always put the date in the same format. It takes up 1.5-2 centimeters, unlike the more familiar format with dots.
