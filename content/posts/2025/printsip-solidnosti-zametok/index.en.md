When I was just getting into note-taking, I kept hearing the advice to make notes atomic.

I think this principle should come together with the principle of solidity: each individual note should be something in its own right. Something valuable to me as the note's author.

Because right now I have quite a few old notes consisting of just one sentence. I look at them and think "so what?!"

So now I try not to split everything up as much as possible, but rather, on the contrary, to merge everything as much as possible within a single note.
